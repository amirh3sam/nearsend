From iPhone to Windows

  1. Keep Nearsend running on your laptop.
  2. Connect your iPhone and laptop to the same trusted Wi-Fi.
  3. Scan the QR code with your iPhone camera, or open the displayed address in Safari and enter the pairing code.
  4. Drop files or folders, or tap Choose files. Paste a note or link and tap Share text.
  5. Preview photos and videos inline, download individual files or a ZIP archive, or copy shared text with one tap.

On Windows, allow Node.js through the firewall on private networks if asked. If you have a VPN or virtual adapters, pick your Wi-Fi adapter from the Network adapter dropdown.

Optional HTTPS: place cert.pem and key.pem in data/ (or set NEARSEND_CERT and NEARSEND_KEY) and restart.
